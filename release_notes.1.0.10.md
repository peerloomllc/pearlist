## What's Changed

### Bug Fixes

- **PearList would not open on some phones.** The app clears out old data in the background to save space, and it was removing a little too much. On a phone where that happened, opening PearList gave a black screen, or a black screen with a spinner that never stopped. Restarting the phone did not help, and neither did clearing the app's cache. This is fixed, so it cannot happen again.

- **If a space still will not open, PearList now tells you and offers to fix it.** Before, a space it could not open looked like a space with nothing in it, which is alarming and easy to misread as your lists having been deleted. It now says what happened and what to do about it. Your lists were never deleted.

- **The app no longer sits on a spinner with nothing to say.** If the part of PearList that holds your lists fails to start, you get a screen explaining that, with a button to copy the details so you can send them to us.

### New Features

- **Rebuild a space from another phone in your household.** If a space on this phone will not open, PearList offers to copy the lists back from someone else's phone. Open PearList on their phone, leave it on screen, and tap Rebuild. It tries a plain retry first, since a space is sometimes just slow to open and that is usually all it needs.

### Improvements

- **Tapping a reminder takes you to the item.** It used to open the right list and leave you to find the thing you were reminded about, which on a long shopping list is most of the point of the reminder. Now it scrolls to the item and highlights it. If someone has already ticked it off or deleted it, the list still opens and a message says why nothing is highlighted.
