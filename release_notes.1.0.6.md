## What's Changed

### New

- Use PearList on more than one phone. Link a second phone from Settings and it becomes *you*, not a new person: your spaces, your name and anything assigned to you show up on both.
- Your spaces follow you. Join a space on one phone and your other phone picks it up on its own.
- One name, everywhere. Set your name once and it stays yours on every phone you have, instead of changing depending on which one you last used.
- Save a copy of a space to a file, and open it back later. Handy before wiping a phone or handing one on.
- Remove a phone you no longer have. Take it off your account and it stops seeing your lists.

### Improvements

- Removing someone from a space now removes every phone they have, not just the one you tapped.
- People show up once in the member list, however many phones they use, and assigning an item reaches all of them.
- Give each of your phones a name, so the list of your devices is readable.
- Settings sections fold away, so the screen is shorter and easier to scan.
- If a pairing was left half-finished, you get a clear "Start over" instead of a stuck screen.
- A smaller download and a faster start.

### Security

- Your recovery phrase now lives in your phone's own secure keystore, and is kept out of Android's cloud backup.
- A phone you remove loses its key to the space, so it stops reading new changes rather than just disappearing from the list.

### Bug Fixes

- PearList could open to a black screen and stay there. It now recovers by itself.
- A background error could close the app. It no longer does.
- Your list of spaces now refreshes straight after linking or removing a phone.
- The messages around removing a person or a phone say what actually happens, and no longer promise more than they do or contradict the result.
- If you are the owner and using your second phone, you can now manage the space from it too.
- An empty space now explains why it looks empty, and you can leave one that will not sync.
