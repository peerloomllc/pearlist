## What's Changed

### New

- **Tick it off and it's gone.** A new setting makes items leave the list the moment you check them, instead of sitting there with a line through them. A list you shop every week stays as short as what is still to get, however many times you have used it.
- It's off unless you turn it on, in Settings under "Lists & aisles". Nothing about your lists changes until you ask for it.
- Ticked the wrong thing? You get a few seconds to undo, and the item comes back unticked, ready to buy again.
- Chore lists are left alone, so you can still tick the chores off and reset them for next week.

Suggested by a PearList user in Vienna, whose weekly shopping list only ever got longer. Thank you.
