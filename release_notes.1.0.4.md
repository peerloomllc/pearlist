## What's Changed

### New

- Tidy finished aisles: tick off an aisle's last item and it folds away. Off by default, in Settings.

### Improvements

- Better aisle sorting: 346 more brand names, plus fixes for items landing in the wrong aisle.
- A lighter app: less to download and less space used on your phone.

### Removed

- Local AI. It found the right aisle under half the time and cost a 0.8 GB download. Sorting is faster and more accurate without it, and that 0.8 GB is deleted for you.

### Bug Fixes

- Settings now shows the version of PearList you are actually running. It had said 0.0.1 since the very first release.
